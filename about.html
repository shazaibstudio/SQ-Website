Who We Are

Founded in 2025 in Lahore by Shazaib Qaiser, SQ Interactive is an interdisciplinary studio merging XR, AI, and cinematic visuals to build immersive property experiences — from WebGL walkthroughs to full VR staging and bespoke branded experiences.

We partner with developers, hotels, architects and real-estate teams to produce high-fidelity assets that reduce time-to-sale and create memorable buyer journeys.

What sets us apart

  • — Premium visual craft: cinematic lighting, PBR materials, photoreal renders.
  • — Fast cross-platform delivery: WebGL, mobile, PC EXE, and VR headsets.
  • — Data & analytics: measure engagement and convert viewer interest into leads.

What We Do

VR Real Estate

High-fidelity VR builds for headsets and exhibition demos — perfect for sales centers & expos.

Interactive Walkthroughs (WebGL)

Mobile and browser-ready WebGL walkthroughs that feel native on phones and desktops.

360° Tours & Video

Furniture Scanning & VR Staging
