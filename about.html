Who We Are

Founded in 2025 in Lahore by Shazaib Qaiser, SQ Interactive merges XR, AI, and cinematic visuals to build immersive property experiences — from WebGL walkthroughs to full VR staging.

We partner with developers, hotels, architects, and real-estate teams to produce high-fidelity assets that reduce time-to-sale and create memorable buyer journeys.

📈 Completed 15+ VR real estate projects — boosting client engagement by 30% on average.
