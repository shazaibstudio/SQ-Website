Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VR real estate tours in Pakistan

Pricing & Packages

How much do VR real estate tours cost in Pakistan?

Our services start from 30,000 PKR for 360° tours, 95,000 PKR for interactive walkthroughs, and 270,000 PKR for full VR experiences. Final pricing depends on property size, complexity, and specific requirements.

What's included in the base price?

Base packages include professional photography/scanning, post-processing, interactive hotspots, web hosting, and shareable links. Additional features like custom branding or advanced interactions are available as add-ons.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment options including 50% upfront and 50% on delivery for projects over 100,000 PKR. Contact us to discuss payment terms that work for your budget.

Timeline & Process

How long does it take to create a VR property tour?

Timeline varies by service: 360° tours take 1 week, interactive walkthroughs take 2-3 weeks, and full VR experiences take 4-6 weeks from initial consultation to final delivery.

What's your typical workflow?

1) Initial consultation and site visit, 2) Photography/scanning session, 3) Post-processing and development, 4) Client review and revisions, 5) Final delivery and hosting setup.

Can you work with tight deadlines?

Yes, we offer rush delivery for urgent projects. Rush orders may incur additional fees but we can often deliver 360° tours within 3-5 days when needed.

Technology & Requirements

Do I need special equipment to view VR tours?

No special equipment needed! Our tours work on smartphones, tablets, computers, and VR headsets. We optimize for web browsers so clients can view instantly without downloads.

What technology do you use for VR development?

We use Unity 3D, Unreal Engine, and WebGL for development. For capture, we use professional Insta360 cameras and 3D scanning equipment to ensure highest quality results.

Can tours be embedded on my website?

Absolutely! We provide embed codes that work with any website platform. Tours are also shareable via direct links, QR codes, and social media.

Services & Coverage

Which areas in Pakistan do you serve?

We're based in Lahore and primarily serve Punjab region including DHA, Bahria Town, and major developments. We also travel to Islamabad and other cities for larger projects.

What types of properties do you work with?

We work with residential properties, commercial spaces, hotels, showrooms, and off-plan developments. From single rooms to entire buildings - we scale to your needs.

Do you provide training on using VR tours?

Yes! We provide training sessions for your sales team on how to effectively use VR tours for client presentations and lead generation.
