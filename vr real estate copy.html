About SQ Interactive

Innovating the future of immersive media through VR, AI, and multimedia storytelling.

Our Story

Founded in 2025 by Shazaib Qaiser in Lahore, Pakistan, SQ Interactive started with a vision to merge cutting-edge VR and AI with creative storytelling. Today, we pioneer immersive experiences that redefine real estate, design, and high-end media.

Founded: 2025 Location: Lahore, PK

Our Mission

To empower industries through immersive media that transforms imagination into reality.

Our Vision

To lead the global market in VR and AI-driven experiences, shaping how people interact with digital spaces.
