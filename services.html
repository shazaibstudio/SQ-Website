Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a project take?

Small 360° tours: 5–7 days. WebGL walkthroughs: 2–4 weeks. Full VR staging: 4–6 weeks.

What do you need from us to get started?

Floor plans, photos, or CAD files. For staging/redesign, style preferences help.

Do I need special hardware?

No. WebGL and 360° tours work on any modern phone or browser. VR headsets are optional.

Can these experiences be embedded on my website?

Yes. WebGL and 360° tours can be embedded directly or hosted by us with analytics.

How do payments work?

50% advance, 50% on delivery. Custom milestone payments available for enterprise clients.

Will this work offline?

Yes. PC builds (EXE) and VR headset builds run offline, which makes them ideal for exhibitions, sales centers, and property expos.

Do you offer discounts or pilot packages?

Yes. For first-time clients and portfolio projects, we offer pilot discounts. Bulk packages for developers are also available.

How much do your services cost?

Packages start at PKR 50,000 for 360° tours and go up depending on interactivity, staging, and VR. Full pricing bands are listed above under each service.
