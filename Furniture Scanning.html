AI Interior Design System (Full VR Styling)

Experience the future of spatial design with our AI-powered interior transformation system, built entirely in 3D.

Users can dynamically change wall materials, lighting, layouts, and furniture styles — all in real time. The AI analyzes spatial context and user prompts to generate personalized interiors instantly.

Built for VR using Unity + C#, this system integrates AI-driven presets and interactions for the Meta Quest 3, enabling immersive hands-on interior design.

ENGINE: Unity 2023 LTS + XR Toolkit

PLATFORM: PCVR Demo, Meta Quest 3 (In Progress)

AI FEATURES: Wall & Floor Materials, Lighting, Layout Optimization

TECH STACK: Unity, GPT-4 API, AI Material Generator
