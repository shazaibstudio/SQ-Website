Overview

Let Guests Explore Before They Book

Hospitality clients were losing bookings to competitors who offered better online previews. Static photos weren't enough — guests wanted to feel the space before committing. The solution: a full 360° virtual tour suite, shot on-site with the Insta360 X3, edited with interactive hotspots, and embedded directly on the client's booking website.

Each tour covers every key space: lobby, rooms, restaurant, pool, and outdoor areas. Guests can navigate freely, click hotspots for room details and pricing, and jump between floors using an interactive floor plan. The result: measurable increases in booking conversion for every client who deployed a tour.

Production Process

From Shoot to Live Site

01 · Scout

Location Scouting & Shot Planning

Visited each property to plan shooting positions — identifying the optimal spots for each room to maximise spatial impression. Scheduled shoots around natural light and guest activity.

02 · Shoot

Insta360 X3 Capture

Shot all spaces with the Insta360 X3 on a tripod at eye level. Multiple exposures per room for HDR blending. Captured both photo and video 360° content for different use cases.

03 · Edit

Post-Production & Hotspot Editing

Stitched and colour-graded all panoramas. Added interactive hotspots for room info, pricing, and navigation. Built floor plan overlays for multi-floor properties.

04 · Deploy

Web Embedding & Integration

Embedded tours directly into client websites using iframe and JavaScript APIs. Integrated with booking engines so guests can go from tour to reservation in one click.
