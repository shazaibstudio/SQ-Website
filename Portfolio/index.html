Who I Am

From Floor Plans
to Full Reality

I'm a VR developer and creative technologist based in Lahore, Pakistan — and founder of SQ Interactive. My work sits at the intersection of immersive 3D, product design, and full-stack web delivery, all shipped for real clients with measurable results.

I hold a BSc in Multimedia Technology with VR/AR specialism from Asia Pacific University in Kuala Lumpur. Before founding my own studio, I designed enterprise interfaces at NetSol Technologies — one of Pakistan's flagship NASDAQ-listed software firms — improving user task efficiency by 20%.

Today I build Meta Quest 3-ready VR environments, design booking engines that process real payments, and produce 360° property tours that help hospitality clients close deals faster. I'm actively seeking full-time roles in Malaysia, the Gulf, or remote where immersive tech is taken seriously.
