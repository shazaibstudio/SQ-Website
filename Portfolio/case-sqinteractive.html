Overview

Building the Studio That Builds Everything Else

SQ Interactive is my creative technology studio. Before I could take on client work, I needed a brand and web presence that demonstrated exactly what I could do — not just described it. The website had to be the portfolio piece itself.

I designed the full brand identity from scratch: logo, colour system, typography, and visual language. Then built the website in clean HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks, no templates. The result is a fast, distinctive, conversion-focused studio site that has directly generated client enquiries.
