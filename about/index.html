Who We Are

A founder-led Digital Technology Partner.

SQ Interactive is based in Lahore, Pakistan. We work alongside businesses to understand what is actually getting in the way, identify where technology can create value, and build practical solutions around those needs.

That can mean a better website, a digital system, an AI workflow, business automation, custom software, an immersive experience — or something that doesn't fit neatly into a category yet.

We don't start with a technology and look for somewhere to use it. We start with the problem.

Why SQ Exists

Bridging the gap between business and technology.

Technology moves quickly. For a business, keeping up with every new platform, tool, AI model, automation system, or digital trend can create as many questions as answers.

What should we actually use? What is worth building? What will make a difference? What will simply add complexity?

SQ Interactive exists to bridge that gap. Our purpose is to make modern technology practical, accessible, and valuable for businesses — helping organizations use technology effectively without having to become technology experts themselves.

We believe technology should solve business problems, not create new ones.

Our Positioning

Not just another technology vendor.

SQ Interactive is not built around selling isolated services. We see ourselves as a technology partner.

That means understanding the business before recommending a solution, being honest about what is and isn't necessary, and staying involved when a project needs improvement, maintenance, training, or continued technical support.

Our positioning is deliberately technology-agnostic. We don't recommend a technology because it is popular. We recommend what makes sense for the problem.

Our Story

From one laptop to a technology company.

SQ Interactive began from a small room in Lahore. The company was built through experimentation — combining design, development, 3D, immersive technology, and emerging AI systems to see what could actually be made.

The early work was heavily focused on websites, immersive experiences, digital media, and visualization. Those projects became the foundation for the broader technology capabilities SQ is developing today.

V1

Foundation

Websites, immersive technologies, digital media, and technical experimentation. Establishing the initial portfolio.

V2

Technology Partner

Expanding beyond specialist services into a broader Digital Technology Partner capable of solving different kinds of business challenges.

V3

Future

Software platforms, AI products, automation systems, immersive technologies, research, and intellectual property for global markets.

Where We're Going

Built in Pakistan. Thinking beyond it.

SQ Interactive is based in Lahore. Our foundation is local, but our ambitions are not limited by geography. The long-term direction is to grow from a founder-led technology partner into an internationally recognized technology company.

Over time, we intend to develop software products, AI systems, automation, immersive technologies, research & innovation, and intellectual property for wider markets.

The objective is not growth for its own sake. It is to build a company capable of creating technology that has lasting value.
